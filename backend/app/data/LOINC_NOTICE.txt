loinc_subset.csv contains a small, hand-curated subset of LOINC codes and their Long Common Names
(the "code" and "display" columns), plus an "abbreviations" column written for this project
(abbreviations are NOT LOINC content).

This material contains content from LOINC (http://loinc.org). LOINC is copyright Regenstrief
Institute, Inc. and the Logical Observation Identifiers Names and Codes (LOINC) Committee and is
available at no cost under the license at http://loinc.org/license. LOINC is a registered
United States trademark of Regenstrief Institute, Inc.

Before any clinical use, verify every code and display against the current official LOINC
release, and re-read the LOINC licence terms for your use case. To use the full LOINC table,
download it from loinc.org (free account) and load it in the same CSV format.
